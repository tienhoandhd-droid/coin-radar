# Crypto Historical Patterns, Cycles, Seasonality and Risk Characteristics (for Coin Radar scoring)

Research date: 2026-10-19. Research budget: about 20 search/fetch calls, so several sub-questions have only secondary sources. Evidence grades used below: **[EVIDENCE]** = peer-reviewed or dataset-based with a stated method; **[WEAK]** = secondary analysis or a single-source backtest; **[FOLKLORE]** = widely repeated with no robust support.

## 1. Bitcoin halving / 4-year cycle (2012, 2016, 2020, Apr 2024) and the 2024–2026 cycle to date

### Takeaway
Through the 2024 cycle the four-year rhythm still held in **timing**: the peak came about 1,060 days after the prior low and about 530 days after the halving. Its **amplitude** has shrunk a lot. Each peak-to-peak gain has been smaller, and the 2025–26 drawdown is about 50–54%, against 77–85% in earlier cycles. As of late September 2026 BTC had reclaimed its 200-day and 200-week moving averages. K33 suggests the February 2026 low near $60k may have been the bottom. Galaxy's June 2026 view was that the bottom window opens only in late 2026. Treat the cycle as a **weak macro prior**, not a timing signal: n = 4 and the cycle is fading.

### Cited Findings
- Days from halving to cycle peak: about 367 (2012 cycle), about 527 (2016), about 549 (2020), about 534 (2024). Peak-to-peak return multiples fell from about 9,500% (2012 cycle) to about 97% (2024 cycle). — [Spark epoch calculator](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-epoch-return-calculator)
- An alternative count gives 373, 525 and 546 days for the first three cycles. The same source puts the 2018 drawdown at −86.3% and the 2022 drawdown at −76.9%. — [QuantJourney](https://quantjourney.substack.com/p/bitcoin-cycles-a-quantitative-analysis)
- Prior bear drawdowns: −83%, −84%, −77%; the current one was −47% when written (still ongoing). — [BIT Knowledge Hub](https://www.bit.com/knowledge-hub/bitcoin-cycle)
- Glassnode: BTC bear markets have bottomed at slightly smaller drawdowns each cycle: −93% (2011), −84% (2015 and 2018), −75% (March 2020). — [Glassnode, "A Bear of Historic Proportions"](https://research.glassnode.com/2022-bear-of-historic-proportions/)
- Galaxy Research (Alex Thorn, data as of 2026-06-09; republished 2026-06-17):
  - Galaxy says "the four-year cycle remains intact," with narrower amplitude, and that the halving's impact is waning.
  - ATH was $124,824 on 2025-10-06, 1,062 days after the prior low, matching the 2017 and 2021 timing.
  - MVRV at the peak was 2.29. Earlier peaks were 5.91, 4.72 and 2.93.
  - The Pi Cycle top signal did not trigger, the first time this has happened.
  - Prior cycle declines were −85%, −84% and −77%. The current decline was −51%. At day 242 the 2013–15 cycle was at −48%, and the 2017–18 and 2021–22 cycles were near −68%.
  - Bottoms have typically formed about 12–13 months after the peak.
  - Base-case bottom zone: $40k–46k. Applying the old 77–85% declines ($19k–29k) is called "no longer valid."
  - Realized price at the peak was 43.7% of ATH, versus 34.2% (2021), 21.2% (2017) and 16.9% (2013).
  - Gap below cost basis at past bottoms: −44%, −31%, −25%, narrowing each cycle. — [Galaxy via KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/galaxy-research-bitcoin-s-four-year-cycle-remains-valid-halving-rule-losing-impact)
- ATH figures conflict across sources: $124,824 (Galaxy), $126,199 (CoinTelegraph) and $126,296 (K33/FXStreet). The differences are most likely due to different price indices or exchanges. — [CoinTelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/bitcoin-done-85percent-crashes-cathie-wood-34k-target); [FXStreet/K33](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/bitcoin-reclaims-key-moving-averages-altering-bear-cycle-pattern-k33-202609222010)
- 2026 path:
  - BTC fell to about $60k in February 2026. — [KuCoin blog](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/pk-bitcoin-bear-market-2026-analysis)
  - There was a June low near $57.7k–58.5k, then a rebound toward $64–65k in July. — [KuCoin blog](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/is-bitcoin-bear-market-ending-in-2026-h2)
  - Note the conflict: K33 still treats February's roughly $60k as the cycle's maximum drawdown, using a different price index. — [The Block/K33](https://www.theblock.co/post/401991/not-all-200-day-moving-averages-are-equal-k33-argues-februarys-60k-low-still-marks-cycles-maximum-drawdown)
- K33 on the 200-day MA:
  - BTC broke below the 200-day MA in November 2025 and retested it around $82k in May 2026.
  - It spent 189 days below it, versus 96, 132 and 85 days in prior cycles. — [The Block/K33](https://www.theblock.co/post/401991/not-all-200-day-moving-averages-are-equal-k33-argues-februarys-60k-low-still-marks-cycles-maximum-drawdown)
- K33, 2026-09-22:
  - Price was $86,400, about 32% below ATH. Maximum drawdown was 54%, versus 77.5–85% lasting 363–407 days in 2013, 2017 and 2021.
  - BTC reclaimed its 50-, 100-, 200-day and 200-week MAs. "Every instance prior to 2026 occurred after BTC had already made its cycle low."
  - May 2026 saw 127,900 BTC of ETF redemptions over seven weeks, the worst ETF outflow period on record.
  - 7-day volatility was 3.5%. 30-day volatility was 2.2% (daily figures).
  - Fear and Greed Index was 78. — [FXStreet/K33](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/bitcoin-reclaims-key-moving-averages-altering-bear-cycle-pattern-k33-202609222010)
- September 2026 prices: about $76.7k (Sep 2), about $85.7k (Sep 23), about $84.6k (Sep 30). BTC market cap was about $1.33T and ETH about $233B. — [Fortune Sep 2](https://fortune.com/article/price-of-bitcoin-09-02-2026/); [Fortune Sep 23](https://fortune.com/article/price-of-bitcoin-09-23-2026/); [Fortune Sep 30](https://fortune.com/article/price-of-bitcoin-09-30-2026/)
- A competing four-year-cycle projection (June 2026) put a possible 75% drawdown to about $33k, bottoming around October 2026. This is a forecast, not evidence. — [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/bitcoin-four-year-cycle-btc-risks-75-drawdown-with-four-months-of-bear-market-still-ahead-202606291041)

### Inferences
- **Real vs coincidental:** halving-to-peak timing was remarkably consistent (about 370–550 days) across 4 cycles. With only 4 observations, statistical significance is impossible, and the timing may reflect a self-fulfilling consensus as much as supply mechanics. Label: **[WEAK]** as a regime prior. Treating it as a timing signal is **[FOLKLORE]**.
- **Fading:** the evidence is consistent. Peak multiples shrank, MVRV at the peak shrank, drawdown depth shrank (−85% → −77% → about −52–54%), and ETF flows have become a new dominant driver.
- For the scoring engine: BTC's cycle phase (drawdown from ATH, position versus 200-day and 200-week MAs) is better used as a **market-wide risk multiplier** than as an entry signal.

### Gaps
- No live price or source for 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-19 was found. The current price, and whether a new low below $57–60k occurred in October 2026, are unverified.
- No peer-reviewed statistical test of the halving effect was found within budget.

## 2. Seasonality: day-of-week, hour-of-day, weekend, month-of-year, turn-of-month

### Takeaway
Calendar effects in crypto are **mostly weak, sample-dependent and not robust out-of-sample**. The most consistent finding concerns **volatility and volume** (lower on weekends and between 00:00 and early-morning UTC, higher during US equity hours), not **returns**. Use seasonality for volatility and liquidity adjustments, not as a directional score.

### Cited Findings
- **Day-of-week, Caporale & Plastun:** no day-of-week effect for LTC, XRP or DASH. Only BTC showed significantly higher Monday returns. Not an out-of-sample design. — [Quantpedia summary](https://quantpedia.com/the-day-of-the-week-effect-in-the-crypto-currency-market/)
- **Day-of-week, out-of-sample (Financial Innovation, 2023):**
  - Method: feedforward neural networks on daily closes from January 2018 to May 2022, evaluated with out-of-sample R² and the Diebold–Mariano test.
  - Result: a day-of-week anomaly for BTC, but none for ETH or ADA. — [Financial Innovation 10.1186/s40854-023-00499-x](https://dx.doi.org/10.1186/s40854-023-00499-x)
- **Kinateder & Papavassiliou (Finance Research Letters, 2019):** no classical day-of-week effect in BTC returns. Weekend risk is significantly lower and early-week volatility more intense. They find a "reverse January effect" and a large drop in risk in September. — [UCD repository](https://researchrepository.ucd.ie/entities/publication/ea776b97-3521-4fce-8a02-c5a9939b5563)
- **Calendar effects, BTC/ETH/XRP (master's thesis):** the effect was weak or absent in its sample. — [VSB thesis](https://dspace.vsb.cz/handle/10084/148743?show=full)
- **Hour-of-day (Quantpedia, Gemini hourly data 2015–2022):**
  - No hour is statistically negative. The worst hours are 03:00–04:00 UTC.
  - When the NYSE is closed (weekends, holidays), BTC returns are concentrated intraday. On NYSE days the pattern reverses.
  - A suggested 21:00–23:00 UTC long rule shows a 33% annualized return. This is a single in-sample backtest. — [Quantpedia intraday](https://quantpedia.com/are-there-seasonal-intraday-or-overnight-anomalies-in-bitcoin/)
- **Activity patterns:**
  - Lower weekend volume and a higher Monday return effect.
  - Exchange activity is higher during US stock-market hours and lower from midnight to early morning.
  - USD-denominated exchanges show stronger patterns than JPY or CNY ones. — [Quantpedia calendar effects](https://quantpedia.com/are-there-any-simple-calendar-effects-in-bitcoin-market/)
- **Turn-of-month:** Kumar (2022, *Managerial Finance*) finds turn-of-month returns positive and significantly higher than other days for BTC, ETH and LTC, August 2015 to August 2021. — [Emerald](https://emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/MF-02-2022-0084/full/html)
- **Month-of-year:** for BTC, July 2010 to September 2019, a May and November effect at only the 10% significance level. — [Applied Finance Letters](https://ojs.aut.ac.nz/applied-finance-letters/article/download/266/85)
- Further FRL papers exist but were not read: Kaiser (2019) "Seasonality in cryptocurrencies" (FRL vol. 31) and Mueller (2024) "Revisiting seasonality in cryptocurrencies" (FRL vol. 64). — referenced in [search summary / IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/ahs/journl/v9y2024i1p43-60.html)

### Inferences
- Day-of-week and month-of-year **return** effects are **[FOLKLORE / WEAK]**. They appear in some samples and vanish in others, and the significance is marginal (for example, 10%).
- Lower weekend and early-Asia-hour **volume and volatility** is **[EVIDENCE]** and useful for Short-term mode. Example uses: widen slippage or spread assumptions, or require higher volume confirmation for breakouts that fire on weekends or between 00:00 and 06:00 UTC.
- Turn-of-month has one supportive paper (to 2021). It has not been confirmed out-of-sample here, so grade it **[WEAK]**.
- Popular "Uptober" or "Sell in May" narratives have no robust support in the sources found. Grade them **[FOLKLORE]**.

### Gaps
- No post-2022 out-of-sample replication of hour-of-day effects was found. ETF-era (2024+) changes to the intraday pattern, such as US-open flows, are not documented here.
- The full findings of Mueller (2024) were not read.

## 3. Drawdown statistics, recovery and survivorship

### Takeaway
BTC bear drawdowns have run from −93% (2011) to about −52–54% (2025–26). ETH fell more than 80% in 2022. Major altcoins routinely fall 80–90%+. Survivorship is very poor:
- Only 9 of the December 2016 top-100 coins were still in the top 100 in 2024.
- 36% of the 2017 top 100 dropped out of the top 100 within one year.
- CryptoRank estimates a 62% chance that a coin entering the top 100 "dies" within 5 years.
- 53% of all tokens tracked by GeckoTerminal since 2021 are dead.

### Cited Findings
- BTC bear lows: −93% (2011), −84% (2015, 2018), −75% (March 2020). — [Glassnode](https://research.glassnode.com/2022-bear-of-historic-proportions/)
- BTC 2022 drawdown −76.9%; 2018 −86.3%. — [QuantJourney](https://quantjourney.substack.com/p/bitcoin-cycles-a-quantitative-analysis)
- BTC 2025–26 drawdown: −51% (Galaxy, June 2026) or −54% (K33, September 2026). Past bears lasted 363–407 days. — [Galaxy via KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/galaxy-research-bitcoin-s-four-year-cycle-remains-valid-halving-rule-losing-impact); [FXStreet/K33](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/bitcoin-reclaims-key-moving-averages-altering-bear-cycle-pattern-k33-202609222010)
- ETH was down over 80% from ATH in 2022. — [Glassnode × CoinMarketCap](https://research.glassnode.com/gn-cmc-report-1/)
- As of January 2022, several altcoins such as UNI and AAVE were already nearing 80% drawdowns from their 2021 highs. — [K33](https://k33.com/research/archive/articles/blood-in-the-streets-crypto-market-cap-slashed-in-half-since-november)
- Alts reportedly dropped "on average over 92%" in the 2018 bear. This is an unsourced single-analyst claim. — [TradingView idea](https://cn.tradingview.com/chart/TOTAL3/KXcD3K8H-Altcoin-Extermination-Worst-Case-Scenario-Explained)
- Of the top 100 coins at December 2016, only 9 were still in the top 100 in 2024 (University of Vaasa thesis, CoinMarketCap data). — [Vaasa](https://osuva.uwasa.fi/bitstreams/2a766d58-9fd3-44b8-b1a3-14a048a0b653/download)
- Van Tharp: 36 of the end-2017 top 100 were out of the top 100 by end-2018. 12 were no longer in the top 200 (10) or completely gone (2). — [Van Tharp](https://vantharp.com/cryptoasset-update-what-happened-to-the-top-100-in-market-cap-on-dec-31st-2016-during-2017-by-van-k-tharp-ph-d/)
- CryptoRank: a coin entering the top 100 has a 62% chance of "dying" within 5 years. "Dead" means delisted from major exchanges, or daily volume below $10k for more than 90 days. — [DigitalToday](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/90791/crypto-top-100-market-cap-tokens-62-percent-disappear-within-5-years)
- CoinGecko dead-coins study (January 2026):
  - 11.6M of about 20.2M projects launched (July 2021 to end-2025) are dead, i.e. 53.2%.
  - Q4 2025 alone accounted for 7.7M failures (34.9% of all failures since 2021). Q1 2025 had 1.8M.
  - Causes cited: meme coin collapse and Pump.fun launches.
  - Method: a token counts as failed if it had at least one trade and then stopped trading; for Pump.fun, only graduated tokens were counted. — [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/news/coingecko-reports-record-token-mortality-in-2025); [CoinTelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/crypto-token-failures-soar-quarter-die-q1-coingecko)

### Inferences
- For a top-1000 universe, survivorship bias is severe. Any backtest of signals on coins *currently* in the top 1000 will overstate returns.
- A coin's tier rank is unstable. About a third of the top 100 rotates out each year in bear years.
- Suggested risk prior for drawdowns in a full bear market:
  - BTC: 50–85%.
  - ETH: 80–95%.
  - Large-cap alts: 80–90%.
  - Mid and small caps: 90%+, with a material chance of never recovering.
- The ETH and alt ranges are partly inferred because primary medians were not found.

### Gaps
- No dataset-based median drawdown by market-cap tier (top 10 / 11–100 / 101–1000) for 2018 or 2022 was found.
- ETH's 2018 drawdown figure (commonly cited as about −94%) was not verified with a source.
- No direct statistic was found on what fraction of the 2021 top 100 or top 1000 is still ranked in 2026, nor on recovery-to-ATH rates for altcoins.

## 4. Volatility statistics, clustering and fat tails (for ATR% risk thresholds)

### Takeaway
BTC's annualized volatility has roughly halved since 2021: about 81% in 2021, about 35% in 2024 and about 42% in 2025. Its ATR/price in 2025 was about 3.4%. ETH's volatility is about 1.3–1.8x BTC's. Returns are strongly fat-tailed: hourly excess kurtosis is about 30. Volatility clusters strongly, so recent ATR% is a valid input for a risk rating.

### Cited Findings
- Schwab, BTC 2025:
  - Historical volatility 42%, maximum drawdown 32%, ATR/P 3.4%. For comparison, TSLA 63%, NVDA 50%, silver 38%.
  - BTC volatility fell about 50% from 2021 to 2025.
  - The ETH/BTC volatility ratio rose from 1.32 (2021) to 1.78 (2025). — [Schwab](https://advisorservices.schwab.com/story/bitcoin-volatility-shrinks-to-magnificent-7-levels)
- Annualized volatility in 2021: BTC 81%, ETH 107%; SOL was the most volatile of the coins covered. — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/chart/amp/27577/cryptocurrency-volatility-dmo)
- BTC's 10-year average annualized volatility is 46.31%; 2024 was 35.48%. Over 180% in 2013 versus as low as 23% in 2025 (aggregator figures, unverified). — [Spark volatility tracker](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-volatility-tracker)
- Kaiko: altcoin hourly intraday volatility (annualized) stayed below 200% after the February 2025 selloff, then spiked above 600% for ADA on the US strategic reserve news (March 2025). — [Kaiko Research](https://research.kaiko.com/insights/altcoin-volatility-surges-on-strategic-reserve-news)
- K33, September 2026: BTC 7-day volatility 3.5% and 30-day 2.2% (daily standard deviation). — [FXStreet/K33](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/bitcoin-reclaims-key-moving-averages-altering-bear-cycle-pattern-k33-202609222010)
- Hourly BTC excess kurtosis is about 29.7. Kurtosis rises as the sampling interval shrinks, and daily returns show volatility clustering. — [arXiv 2010.07402](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.07402)
- BTC shows fat tails, volatility clustering and aggregational Gaussianity (Physica A, 2020). — [da Cunha & da Silva](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/phsmap/v550y2020ics0378437120300133.html)
- Strong excess kurtosis at 1-minute data; fat tails and volatility clustering confirmed. — [arXiv 1707.07618](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.07618)
- Generic tier definitions: large cap >$10B, mid cap $1–10B, small cap $100M–1B, micro cap <$100M. No volatility numbers are given. — [Kryptex](https://pool.kryptex.com/articles/market-cap-en)

### Inferences
- Suggested starting thresholds for **daily ATR% (14-day)**. These are calibration anchors, not sourced cut-offs:
  - Low: ≤3–4%, i.e. BTC-like (BTC 2025 ATR/P was 3.4%, about 42% annualized).
  - Medium: about 4–6%, i.e. ETH-like (about 1.3–1.8x BTC).
  - High: about 6–10%.
  - Very high: >10%.
- Daily volatility of about 2.2–3.5% (K33) corresponds to roughly 42–67% annualized (×√365).
- Because of fat tails (kurtosis about 30 hourly), stops at k×ATR will be hit far more often than a Gaussian model implies. Short-term mode on 1H/4H data needs wider multiples, or a cap on position risk.
- Volatility clustering means the latest 7–14 day ATR% is a reasonable forward risk estimate. A risk tier should still be bumped up after volatility spikes, since spikes persist.

### Gaps
- No primary source gives realized volatility by market-cap tier (top 10 / 11–100 / 101–1000). The thresholds above are extrapolated and should be calibrated on Coin Radar's own data: compute the ATR% distribution per tier over 2022–2026.

## 5. New listings: returns over the first 30/90/180 days

### Takeaway
The evidence consistently shows a short pop around listing, then strong underperformance over months. Among 2024 Binance listings, the median return was about +3% at 30 days and about −60% at six months. Only about 10–16% were above their listing price after six months.

### Cited Findings
- 4Pillars (October 2024), 32 tokens listed on Binance in 2024:

  | Horizon | Median return | Share positive |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 day | +1.0% | 53.1% |
  | 30 days | +2.9% | 50.0% |
  | 6 months | −60.0% | 9.5% |
  | YTD (to Oct 28, 2024) | −56.4% | 12.5% |

  - Over the same YTD period BTC was +60%. Only memecoins and BANANA outperformed BTC.
  - The reference price used is not specified. — [4Pillars](https://research.4pillars.io/en/research/binance-listing-flash-or-fade)
- Flow / tradetheflow (May 2024): only 16% of 31 tokens traded above their listing price after six months. — [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/binance-effect-fades-less-than-20-tokens-are-profitable-six-months-after-listing-202405201006)
- A study of 389 tokens listed in 2024 across six exchanges: Binance listings pumped about 87% on average at listing, then fell about 70% from the listing price. The "98% dump" headline is from a press summary. — [BitPinas](https://bitpinas.com/cryptocurrency/binance-token-listing-dump/)
- Other estimates: Animoca reportedly found a −49% average after CEX listings; an older CoinDesk-cited figure claimed a +41% listing jump. Results depend on window and method. — [RockawayX](https://rockawayx.com/insights/bullish-or-bearish-what-really-happens-when-tokens-list-on-a-cex); [The Big Whale](https://www.thebigwhale.io/articles-en/listing-on-binance-what-impact-on-the-price-of-tokens)

### Inferences
- **[EVIDENCE, moderate]** Tokens younger than about 180 days have a strongly negative expected drift over 1–6 months. Several independent analyses agree, though the samples are small (about 30–400 tokens) and come mostly from 2024, a period of low-float launches.
- For the scoring engine:
  - Apply an age penalty: token age < 90–180 days → raise the risk tier by at least one level.
  - In Swing mode, down-weight bullish signals on recent listings.
  - The first 1–30 days are close to a coin flip (about 50% positive), so short-term breakout signals there are noise plus risk.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed study with 90-day horizons was found, nor any 2025–2026 cohort.
- Airdrop-token-specific statistics were not found within budget.

## 6. Low-float / high-FDV as a risk factor

### Takeaway
Binance Research documented the structure: 2024 launches had an average float of about 13% and an average MC/FDV of 12.3%, with about $155B of unlocks due from 2024 to 2030. The **return** evidence linking low float to underperformance is largely anecdotal or overlapping with the listing studies above. It is directionally strong and widely accepted, but no rigorous cross-sectional study was found.

### Cited Findings
- Binance Research (2024):
  - Newly launched tokens had circulating supply as low as 6%, none above 20%, averaging 13%.
  - 2024 launches averaged MC/FDV of 12.3%, which implies about $80B of new demand would be needed to hold prices.
  - About $155B of token unlocks are due from 2024 to 2030. — [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/binance-highlights-155-billion-token-unlock-overhang/); [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/binance-doubles-down-on-low-float-high-fdv-calls-for-action/)
- Binance changed its listing criteria toward a moderate float at TGE (token generation event) and lower allocations outside the community. — [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/binance-doubles-down-on-low-float-high-fdv-calls-for-action/)
- Analysts disagree on the cause of the underperformance: VC selling versus market conditions. — [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/whos-to-blame-for-the-underperformance-of-low-float-high-fdv-tokens/)

### Inferences
- A low MC/FDV ratio (for example <25–30%) plus upcoming unlocks is a reasonable **[WEAK–moderate]** risk flag. It is supported mechanically (supply overhang) and by the 2024 listing cohort data, which mostly consisted of low-float tokens.
- Suggested rule: MC/FDV < 0.3 → risk +1 tier. A large unlock within the hold window (≤10 days for Swing) → hard warning.

### Gaps
- No regression or cross-sectional study of MC/FDV versus forward returns was found.
- No systematic study of returns around unlock events (e.g. a 30-day window) was found within budget. Sources such as Keyrock or The Tie unlock studies were not reached.

## 7. Regime definitions and simple detection rules

### Takeaway
The best-supported simple regime tool is **price relative to moving averages**. Detzel et al. show that price-to-MA ratios predict daily BTC returns both in- and out-of-sample, and that MA-based strategies beat buy-and-hold on Sharpe. Practitioners also use the 200-day and 200-week MAs and the drawdown from ATH to label bull and bear regimes. These are descriptive conventions, not tested rules.

### Cited Findings
- Detzel, Liu, Strauss, Zhou & Zhu, "Learning and predictability via technical analysis: Evidence from bitcoin and stocks with hard-to-value fundamentals":
  - Price-to-MA ratios predict daily BTC returns in- and out-of-sample.
  - MA strategies earn economically significant alpha and higher Sharpe ratios than buy-and-hold.
  - The working paper tested 5-, 10-, 20-, 50- and 100-day SMAs (hold BTC above the MA, cash below). — [WUSTL profile](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/learning-and-predictability-via-technical-analysis-evidence-from-/); [CXO Advisory](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/currency-trading/timing-bitcoin-with-smas/)
- K33 uses breaks and reclaims of the 200-day and 200-week MAs as regime markers. Time spent below the 200-day MA in bear phases has been 85–189 days. — [The Block/K33](https://www.theblock.co/post/401991/not-all-200-day-moving-averages-are-equal-k33-argues-februarys-60k-low-still-marks-cycles-maximum-drawdown); [FXStreet/K33](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/bitcoin-reclaims-key-moving-averages-altering-bear-cycle-pattern-k33-202609222010)
- Bespoke (via Bloomberg, 2022): forward returns after BTC breaks back above the 200-day MA were stronger than average. This is anecdotal. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-03-07/bitcoin-stuck-in-the-doldrums-is-making-some-bulls-optimistic)
- Galaxy uses the 200-week MA (about $62k in June 2026), realized price or cost basis, and MVRV as cycle anchors. — [Galaxy via KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/galaxy-research-bitcoin-s-four-year-cycle-remains-valid-halving-rule-losing-impact)
- An academic paper on detecting bull and bear phases in crypto in advance exists but was not reviewed. — [arXiv 2411.13586](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.13586)

### Inferences
- A practical regime rule for Coin Radar, combining evidence-backed and convention-backed elements:
  - **Bull:** BTC close > 200-day SMA, and the 50-day SMA > 200-day SMA.
  - **Bear:** BTC < 200-day SMA, and drawdown from ATH > 20%.
  - **Sideways / transition:** everything else.
- Apply the BTC regime as a market-wide multiplier on altcoin long scores. A coin-level price/200-day MA (or 20-week MA) ratio can serve as a per-coin trend filter.
- The "−20% from ATH = bear" convention is borrowed from equities. Crypto routinely has 20–30% corrections inside bull markets (e.g. −53% in mid-2021, per Galaxy). A threshold of 30–35% may fit crypto better, but this is untested.

### Gaps
- No formal backtest with 200-day or 20-week MA drawdown-reduction statistics for BTC or altcoins was found.
- The journal venue and year of Detzel et al. were not verified (possibly *Financial Management*, 2021).
- No academic regime definition (e.g. Pagan–Sossounov or Markov-switching applied to crypto) was reviewed.
